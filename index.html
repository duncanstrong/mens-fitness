Nutrition Tips

  • Protein: 0.8-1g per lb of body weight for muscle building
  • Hydration: Drink at least 3-4 liters of water daily
  • Meal Timing: Eat a balanced meal 2-3 hours before training
  • Post-Workout: Consume protein and carbs within 1-2 hours after training
  • Consistency: Track your food for 2-4 weeks to understand your intake
  • Vegetables: Aim for 2-3 cups daily for micronutrients
